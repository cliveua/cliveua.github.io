Superfolio support

Superfolio shows the portfolios of well-known investors, taken from their public 13F filings with the US Securities and Exchange Commission. If something isn't working or you have a question, write to kettles-netball2z@icloud.com and we will answer as soon as we can.

Common questions

Why is a portfolio out of date? Investment managers file a 13F up to 45 days after each quarter ends, so a portfolio shows what was held at the end of the last quarter. Superfolio checks for new filings twice a day.

Why are some holdings missing? A 13F only lists US-listed shares and options. It leaves out cash, bonds, short positions and most holdings outside the US.

Why did my alert arrive late? Superfolio looks for new filings itself, when you open it and when iOS lets it run in the background. iOS decides when that happens, so an alert can come a few hours after a filing. Make sure notifications are on for Superfolio in Settings.

How do I follow more than three investors? The free plan follows three. Superfolio Plus has no limit. When Plus ends, your extra follows are paused, not deleted, and come back if you subscribe again.

How do I cancel or change Superfolio Plus? In Superfolio, open the You tab and tap Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period to avoid the next charge.

I subscribed on another device. How do I get Plus here? Open the You tab and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I get a refund? Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do you have my data? No. There is no account, and what you follow stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
