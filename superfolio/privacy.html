Superfolio privacy policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

Superfolio is an app for reading the publicly filed stock portfolios of well-known investors. It is made by Clive Ayonye ("we", "us").

The short version: Superfolio has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. We do not collect your name, email address or contacts, and we do not track you.

1. What stays on your device

  • The investors you follow, your choice of light or dark appearance, and whether alerts are on. These are saved on your device only. They are not sent to us, and they are removed when you delete the app.
  • Alerts. Superfolio's alerts are created on your device: the app checks for new filings in the background and shows the notification itself. We do not run a push notification service and we do not hold a notification token for your device.

2. What leaves your device

To show you anything, the app has to request it over the internet. Like every internet request, these carry your IP address and basic technical details such as the app and system version. None of them carry your name, an account, or the list of investors you follow.

Our database host keeps routine server logs of requests, including IP addresses, for a limited time for security and troubleshooting. We do not use these logs to identify you or to build a profile of you.

If you tap a link to an original letter, a filing, or a photo's source page, it opens in your browser and that website's own privacy policy applies.

3. Purchases

Superfolio Plus is bought through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card details or your Apple Account. The app only learns from Apple whether a subscription is active on your device.

4. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking tools in the app.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

5. Children

Superfolio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Your rights and how to reach us

Because we do not hold an account or a profile for you, there is normally nothing for us to look up, correct or delete; removing the app removes what it saved. If you have a question, or believe we hold information about you, write to kettles-netball2z@icloud.com and we will answer within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, for example if we add optional accounts so that your follows sync between devices, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches you.
